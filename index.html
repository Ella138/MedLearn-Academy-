RESEARCH CHECKLIST

Build a responsible project

  • Write one clear research question.
  • Ask a teacher to review your plan.
  • Explain the project and seek appropriate permission before surveying participants.
  • Do not collect names or private medical information.
  • Report the results honestly, including limitations.
  • Never invent data, citations or conclusions.

For a school project, follow your school's research and safeguarding rules. Keep participation voluntary and anonymous where possible.
